Who We Are

Empowering Educators, Elevating Classrooms

Restoring teaching dignity and improving classroom learning.

Founded by visionary educational leader **Hariom Prasad Chourasia** alongside **Sarabjeet Kaur**, the Shikshak Shakti Council is built on the belief that teachers are the primary catalyst of social change. We work directly with primary teachers, educational experts, and public school networks to elevate teaching practices, classroom quality, and educator confidence.

By shifting the focus from passive listening to interactive engagement, we help teachers adapt to modern needs without losing the timeless touch of direct mentoring and empathy.

Our Vision

Confluence of Knowledge, Nature, & Technology

The Philosophy

Three Pillars of Teaching Power

True educational impact occurs when teachers learn to balance internal intellect (Knowledge) with open, stress-free environments (Nature) and modern interactive devices (Technology).

Read Our Manifesto
Knowledge (ज्ञान)

Deep understanding of concept sequences and child psychology. Empowering teachers with comprehensive lesson plans and clear curriculum objectives.

Nature (प्रकृति)

Using natural settings, open air, and hands-on activities. Connecting education directly to children's immediate physical surroundings for real-world relevance.

Technology (तकनीकी)

Integrating digital companion teacher apps and offline multimedia tools to make learning visual, trackable, and scalable across school networks.
